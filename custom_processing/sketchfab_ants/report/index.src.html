Method

How these numbers were made

Specimens

All 135 downloadable ant models from tunosemi on Sketchfab, used with the author's permission (CC BY-NC-ND on Sketchfab). Names are tidied from the Sketchfab titles. The set includes queens, majors, minor workers, three Cretaceous stem ants and two uploads of Blepharidatta conops.

Targets

Blender joined each model's shells into one watertight surface by voxel remeshing, decimated it to about 100,000 vertices and applied very light Laplacian smoothing. Each target was then rotated to the template frame (head +X, back +Z) by the best of 24 axis-aligned rotations.

Fit

fitter_3d/optimise.py with the four stages of ants_cfg.yaml and the SMIL_OmniAnt.pkl template (10,229 vertices, 55 joints, 13 shape coefficients). On a CPU-only Mac the chamfer loss used a dense implementation that matches pytorch3d's values and gradients exactly.

Neutral shapes and PCA

Each fit was re-skinned with all joint rotations set to zero. Its per-vertex deformation was carried back through each vertex's skinning transform; re-skinning reproduces the fitter's vertices exactly. Shapes were centred and scaled to unit centroid size, and the PCA runs on all 10,229 vertices. Scores are in standard deviations.

Error measures

Chamfer: 30,000 surface samples per mesh, in the fitter's frame. Per-vertex and per-region errors: nearest distance to the target surface, divided by body length (the head-to-gaster extent of the fitted body).

Files

Results: tunosemi_ants/fit3d_results_OmniAnt/. Analysis: tools/analyse_fit.py. Figures: tools/build_report_assets.py and render_jobs.py (Blender 4.5).
